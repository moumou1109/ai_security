# 证据卡 · DFEval2024-01 真实场景下开源检测器 AUC 下降 45–50%

## 1. 这条证据说什么

在 2024 年真实传播的深伪（in-the-wild）基准上，开源 SOTA 检测器的 AUC 相对其原始学术基准**下降约 50%（视频）/ 48%（音频）/ 45%（图像）**；多个模型 AUC 接近 0.5，等同随机猜测。

## 2. 原文 / 原始记录

> "We observe an average drop in AUC of 50% for video, 48% for audio, and 45% for image models when evaluated on Deepfake-Eval-2024, as compared to the academic datasets that the models were originally tested on."

> "All off-the-shelf open-source models perform poorly on Deepfake-Eval-2024. The maximum AUC of open-source models across modalities and models was 0.58 … many off-the-shelf models have an AUC close to 0.5, the same as random guessing."
